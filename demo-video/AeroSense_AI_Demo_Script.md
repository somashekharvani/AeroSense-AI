### SCENE 6 — Pollution Forecast and Atmospheric Risk
**Timestamp: 3:50 – 4:45**  
*(Visual: Navigate to `/forecast`. Focus on 48-hour hourly curve and lapse rate gauge)*

> "Next, let's explore Pillar 2: **Pollution Forecast and Atmospheric Risk**.
> 
> Air quality changes dramatically between day and night. During cold winter evenings, the ground cools rapidly, creating a **thermal inversion**. Cold air is trapped beneath a warmer ceiling of air, compressing particulate pollution into a suffocating blanket.
> 
> AeroSense AI's forecasting engine plots a forward **48-hour hourly outlook**. By analyzing vertical temperature gradients between the surface, 925 hPa, and 850 hPa pressure levels, our physical lapse rate engine detects inversion traps hours before they compress ground-level air.
> 
> For our Bengaluru family, the forecast shows that particulate concentrations remain stable throughout the afternoon, with a slight nocturnal dip in the planetary boundary layer around midnight.
> 
> Notice our **Backtest Calibration Panel**. We believe in scientific honesty: we display the Mean Absolute Error, Root Mean Square Error, and systemic bias of our models against historical station observations. We never claim a model is 100% accurate; we show its actual uncertainty."
